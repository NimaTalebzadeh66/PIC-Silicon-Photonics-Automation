\# Chapter 03 — Routing, Connectivity \& PIC Circuit Assembly



\## What I learned



\- Separate component placement from routing.

\- Use `route\_single()` and `route\_bundle()` for optical interconnects.

\- Treat port ordering, orientation, pitch, and path length as circuit-design constraints.



\## Chapter outcome



Built a parameterized 4-channel PIC with:

\- automatic placement and bundle routing

\- top-level optical I/O

\- connectivity manifest

\- routing/port regression tests

\- hierarchical reusable cells
